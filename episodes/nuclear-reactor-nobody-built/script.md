## Three opening hooks
1. **Missing fuel (chosen):** "In nineteen seventy-two, a French lab tested uranium from a mine in Gabon. Some of its fuel was missing."
2. **No builder:** "Two billion years before anyone existed, a rock in Africa ran a nuclear reactor. Nobody built it."
3. **No off switch:** "Every nuclear reactor needs fuel, something to slow its neutrons, and someone to switch it off. This one had no someone."

Hook 1 gives a concrete contradiction in the first sentence and the answer within ten seconds.
