These two spoons can be exactly the same temperature. But metal feels colder. How? Leave metal and wood in the same room long enough, and both settle to room temperature.

Your hand is warmer than the spoon. Touch it, and heat flows out of your skin. Metal carries that energy away faster than wood. Same starting temperature. Different heat transfer.

That colder feeling comes from your skin cooling faster. Wood takes heat away more slowly. Your hand isn't simply reading the spoon's temperature. It's responding to heat leaving your skin.
