# the channel — high-quality 1080p delivery

ADOPTED by owner, 17 October 2026 at13:54 Asia/Manila. Overrides older mandatory 4K language for new production. Historical movies, proofs, source versions and releases retain their original specifications.

## Default final output

- Native1080×1920,9:16,30 fps, progressive. Render images, type, compositing and effects directly at this size. Do not enlarge the 720p review movie and call it a native 1080p final.
- H.264 High Profile MP4, yuv420p, variable bitrate targeting12 Mbps with16 Mbps peak ceiling and24 Mbps buffer. This is a quality/size starting point, not a platform-compression guarantee. Adjust only after actual output inspection shows a need; record any revision.
- AAC stereo 48 kHz 320 kbps, MP4 fast start. Maintain the existing loudness, diction, complete final-word, SFX audibility and music requirements.
- A38-second movie is approximately59MB at the target combined bitrate; complex footage and encoder rate control can vary. Lower pixel count reduces rendering/memory demands, not by a guaranteed wall-clock factor.
- Preserve SDR colour consistently. Explicit colour transforms/tags must agree with the renderer; do not retag source pixels arbitrarily or introduce an HDR workflow.

## What stays mandatory

Complete story and all scenes; image-led visual operations; bold pinned typography; protected captions; strong source-local materials and texture; meaningful grain, focus, camera/transition blur, chromatic treatment, jitter/exposure and local ink finishing where appropriate; complete narration, audible SFX and music; exact established ending; facts/rights; complete encoded quality review; immediate versioned GitHub archiving.1080p is a delivery change, not a reduced craft standard.

Review at phone size and100% delivery scale. Preserve visible paper/print detail; very fine changing grain must be checked for compression mud. Do not silently remove approved materials to reduce file size.

## Reviews, optional formats and4K

720p may be used for a complete early review, labelled REVIEW. A1080p posting export must pass its own full decode/frame/audio and actual encoded visual checks. Approval of a720p preview does not constitute a native 1080p output inspection.

4K remains an optional explicit request or justified delivery requirement, not the default. A change of resolution changes the frozen settings/fingerprint and requires resolution-matched finishing evidence. Never reuse2160×3840 proof as if it tested1080×1920 pixels. WebM/browser exports remain available when requested or needed for an interactive deliverable; they are not mandatory duplicate encodes for every routine posting MP4.

CLI: `pipeline.py render` defaults to `--profile posting` (1080p MP4). `--profile native` explicitly retains the historical4K MP4/WebM/download route. `preview` is review mode and does not bypass the production readiness gates for posting. Both production profiles retain source, sequence, finishing and whole-film approval gates. Frozen package resolution must match the requested production profile.
