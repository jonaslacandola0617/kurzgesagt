# Required image-led finishing output contract

Adopted15 October2026 after owner rejected missing finishing in Wrinkled Fingers R1. This supplements the existing visual/sequence direction; it does not change the Bible's factual, brand or audio rules.

A material_profile string, packaged texture or imported effect is not evidence that the final movie uses it. A new/custom image-led renderer must declare finishing.enabled, a subject-specific temporal/material contract and native runtime proof before a native release. Historical approved source rebuilds retain their exact appearance.

Required proof:
1. Distinct object-local material treatments, including paper/fiber and appropriate image-print/plastic/photocopy interaction. Evaluate actual encoded phone-size output, not only a magnified still or source HTML.
2. Moving grain and material variation visible at phone size; source-dependent coarse/fine scale survives native encoding. Grain must not erase evidence or mimic measured data.
3. Declared camera/transition blur envelopes at actual joins. Finite blur must settle to sharp recognition/reading holds. Source objects exit/occlude correctly before blur is applied.
4. Selective focus follows the selected source detail and uses the same spatial field for surround luminance. A whole-frame blur is not selective focus. Short hunts settle, rather than leaving a whole sequence unfocused.
5. Restrained image chromatic separation, coherent mechanical jitter/exposure; local ink bleed/12fps boil where printed strokes warrant them. Never wobble scientific/photo identity or measurements.
6. Native A/B runtime checks demonstrate effects change picture pixels; captions are composited afterward; the fully shown locked ending is pixel-identical with finishing toggled. Repeating the same frame is deterministic, including grain. A constant-source test in one exposure bucket must show changing grain at phone size; a static JPEG overlay cannot pass.
7. Record hardest join, selected source, settled evidence, material close-up, phone view and exact ending. Technical checks cannot certify the style; internal visual findings remain distinct from owner approval.

Shared implementation: browser/editorial-finish.js reuses filmState/boilPath/drawInkStroke. Wrinkled Fingers R2 shows an original native Canvas adaptation: feathered focus planes, coordinated shading, padded transient defocus/directional integration, RGB channels, timed grain. Its timestamps/subjects belong to that film; future films author their own choreography.

Engine enforcement: validate_finishing in finishing_contract.py is invoked by the production CLI. It rejects undeclared finishing for new image-led artwork builds and absent/failed/mismatched native runtime evidence for native release. A custom capture pipeline must call it too. Runtime evidence pins the rendered studio SHA256 and must be regenerated after changes that affect the studio; encoded phone review follows actual final encoding. Artistic approval and final audio/source/decode checks remain independent.
