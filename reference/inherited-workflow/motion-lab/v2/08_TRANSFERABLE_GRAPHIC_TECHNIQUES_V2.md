## Selection logic for new channel films
Use the **next fact** to choose a technique: a value changing→MG02/MG07; a cause→MG03; a physical scale→MG25; a geographically attached event→MG22–MG23; an evidence quote→MG10; a historic artwork→original/cleared source with MG17 geometry only. Do not reuse the same camera or composition fingerprint every six shots.

## Material limits
The motion forensics focused on **20 source windows**; do not generalize one visual sample to all Vox work. The technique statuses are editorial adoption decisions for *our original engine*, not factual findings about Vox plugins, exact easing or viewer retention.
