**Status: source-audited research, technical original moving rigs checked, governing v1.3 additions evidence-qualified.** Source study dates 11–12 October 2026. This is the second research lab, built on *five user-supplied mixed-media Vox productions*. The v1 lab used primarily third-party Vox-inspired tutorials; it remains valuable but must not be relabeled authentic original Vox.

## The exclusion rule

Study **G** original authored full-screen motion graphics, **O** independently authored tracked overlays, and the **graphics-only portions** of **C** composites. **R** filmed-only segments and **P** underlying archival source photographs, footage, scans and static source map pixels are excluded. X indicates an uncertain composite. A track-following label over filmed imagery may yield an engineering technique, but the filmed subject is never copied, recreated or bundled as a reference asset.

No original Vox video, original drawing/frame, film soundtrack, recorded voice, proprietary source project, photo, historical map plate or brand asset is included in public output. Original test rigs use neutral/new channel geometry and invented data only.
