# the channel — creative visual sequence direction

**ADOPTED by owner · 15 October 2026 Asia/Manila · Bible v1.4.4.** Applies to new films. The owner approved the explanation of the Epigenetics comparison and explicitly requested keeping these sequence requirements in the infrastructure and applying them from now on. This operationalizes continuity of visual thought; existing image-led, brand, audio, factual, caption, ending and native-delivery requirements remain intact.

## 1. Images have an editorial job

Each important narration beat must identify a visual operation: establish, question, inspect, compare, sample, connect, measure, select, remove, reveal or resolve. Define the subject, operation and new understanding before selecting a layout. A beautiful photograph identifying a noun is insufficient where a relationship or mechanism needs explanation.

Image-led means photographic subjects carry the explanation where appropriate. Accurate source-attached or printed scientific figures can show invisible processes. Do not force photographs to prove internal physiology or measured data they do not contain. Paper supports prints, working figures and evidence; it is not the universal scene protagonist.

## 2. Required production order

1. Lock the factual proposition, evidence, uncertainty and viewer's starting assumption.
2. Write at least two distinct visual approaches for a difficult claim; choose for explanatory strength.
3. Design connected recognition, relationship, difficult intermediate, reveal and resolved states. These five jobs are a workshop framework, not a compulsory five-shot formula for every scene.
4. Name the invariant passed between states: subject, orientation, category, source, baseline, question or measurement. Choose meaningful operations and justified transitions before assets.
5. Specify photographic crops, components, masks, hidden regions, local anchors and native detail requirements.
6. Produce finished art with the approved image/material/type craft; review the difficult intermediate, not only the cover.
7. Review the visual explanation without narration, retaining informative visual labels. Ask whether the main relationship/change can be followed. Hide decorative headings separately to check that they are not doing all the explanatory work. Do not expect every scientific detail to be understood without language.
8. Obtain owner art/sequence approval, then animate the hardest representative passage using existing approved assets/audio where suitable.
9. Review actual moving first/intermediate/resolved states, readable holds, caption placement, source registration, complete exits and audible actions. A valid export does not certify creative integration.
10. After moving approval, complete the film and normal technical/factual/audio release gates. Save source/artwork milestones and durable render recovery checkpoints.

## 3. Shot contract fields that must be filled

`viewer_before`, `factual_proposition`, `visual_operation`, `subject_action`, `viewer_after`, `invariant`, `initial_state`, `difficult_intermediate`, `resolved_state`, `next_inheritance`, `representation_reason`, `source_truth_boundary`, `caption_clearance`, `reading_hold`, `owner_review`.

A camera move needs an explanatory purpose: inspect a real feature, reveal a meaningful relation, shift scale or follow an operation. Moving a completed card across the screen does not by itself develop an idea. An appropriate reading hold can be still. Source images and print backings may be transformed separately where truthful; scientific diagrams need independently constructed relevant parts.

## 4. Anti-template checks

Inspect a consecutive shot strip with narration absent. Flag repeated heading + framed photo + annotation arrangements when only the noun changes. Different editorial jobs should normally create different visual hierarchies. Stable layout is appropriate for a progressing comparison or operation; record what changes in knowledge. Do not manufacture arbitrary variety that breaks orientation.

Labels can be a person’s name, question, specimen tag, category, quantity or selected detail. Large headings must not carry every clause of the explanation. Repeated paper stacks, stronger grain, new colours or more parallax cannot compensate for a weak visual operation.

## 5. Independent review gates

Record PER BEAT: source/fact integrity; visual operation clarity; connected-state continuity; image/art/material craft; phone/label clarity; moving integration; audio/caption/export status. Use OWNER PASS / REVISE / NOT REVIEWED with reasons. No average grade hides a failed beat.

The owner's “all looks good to me” response approves the four finished static studies' appearance for the lab. It does not approve their use as universal layouts, the new Recorded Voice beatboard, a completed moving sequence or a replacement channel palette. Finished still treatment stays available; its temporal counterparts require motion review.

## 6. First exercise and future reuse

The authorized first exercise reuses Recorded Voice's cleared assets and Bernard source recording: establish the listener, reveal air/body routes, show microphone capture of the airborne route, retain the absent internal route as a comparison reference, and resolve into speaking versus playback. The exact representation is an original channel proposal, not copied Epigenetics coordinates or its scientific claims.

Review five connected finished states before the short moving test. The short test should be about 10–15 seconds when complete approved narration phrases permit; never clip words or speed the voice to hit a duration. Carry microphone/room qualifications in the complete explanation.

Future science videos use the same decision process with subject-specific operations. They do not reuse this ear/microphone choreography as an intro template. Keep the old headline display face, Manrope center captions, strong local textures, forward existing SFX, Bernard, evolving original music, exact legacy brand ending and all native/final-word safeguards.
