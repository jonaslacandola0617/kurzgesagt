# Visual translation and rendered evidence safeguard

Adopted operational supplement to Bible v1.4.4, 15 October 2026, following owner instruction “okay go”. Existing artistic requirements remain in force.

## Mandatory execution sequence

1. Read the current governing direction and rejection log. Never replace it with a shortened personal summary.
2. For each difficult passage record: specific reference observation, transferable principle, original execution, and actual operation performed by the chosen assets. Reference observations must distinguish finished frames from process demonstrations; never invent a film edit from a process GIF.
3. Build ONE demanding passage as initial, intermediate and resolved finished states before expanding the film. Images must participate in selecting, revealing, comparing, transforming or otherwise explaining the proposition. Choose another construction if only a noun changes between framed photographs.
4. Render and inspect all three states, including a version without decorative headings. Keep informative labels. Compare the actual result with the stated principle and identify remaining weaknesses. Strong texture, typography and parallax do not prove a visual operation.
5. Record render paths, SHA-256 digests, comparison evidence and fallback findings. An unresolved fallback finding blocks motion. Never mark a check clear merely because its text exists.
6. Present the passage for owner art approval. Only recorded approval of this actual version permits animation. Later moving approval remains a separate requirement.

## Fallback rejection

Reject heading + framed photo + arrows when it substitutes for the planned image operation; reject paper-stack variety that leaves the explanation unchanged; reject a beautiful cover with an unresolved intermediate. These devices may be useful in other passages, but they cannot satisfy a different promised operation. Record why a retained device serves the particular proposition.

## Evidence and honesty

Contract checks establish that decisions, evidence references and approval records exist. They cannot certify artistry, factual correctness, a Vox match, authentic file contents or that a human inspected them. Inspect the actual files and preserve their reviewed hashes. Rejected work stays rejected until a new version receives explicit approval. Report static, motion and technical findings separately. Do not claim complete mastery or a percentage match.

## Required contract addition

Every shot requires `visual_translation` with `reference_observation`, `transferable_principle`, `original_execution`, `asset_operation`. Before motion, `rendered_review` requires initial/intermediate/resolved path-and-sha256 records, `heading_hidden_evidence`, `comparison`, `remaining_weaknesses`, and an empty `unresolved_fallbacks` list. Required records supplement all existing sequence and approval fields.
