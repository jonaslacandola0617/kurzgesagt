# Complete Sun-standard acceptance checklist

This expands the previous condensed checklist into every owner-specified item. Copy into each episode; never inherit PASS from the benchmark. The companion ACCEPTANCE_REQUIREMENTS.json gives stable IDs and verification methods.

Record PASS / REVISE / NOT REVIEWED / NOT APPLICABLE (with specific reason), encoded timecode, evidence file, and reviewer. A script intention, source declaration, pixel difference or timing test does not establish subjective success. A longer hold stays an explicit exception; no silent relaxation of the 1.5–2-second target.

Scope: the original list was temporary for the Sun experiment. The later explicit owner adoption made the approved craft the future baseline. Keep the original topic-specific brief and any subsequent owner direction clear. No view-count promise.
