## Licensing/redistribution safety
- Filename prefixes `mixkit-`, `freesound_community-`, `ES_`, and named creators do not grant redistribution permission. Some downloadable libraries license **use in productions** but forbid re-uploading a sound pack. Per-file permissions must be checked before public redistribution.
- The new `audio/sfx_v1/` pack is generated entirely from original waveform formulas using NumPy/SciPy; it does **not** remix or bundle any downloaded third-party SFX.
- Designed tones, mechanical approximations, synthetic air/noise, and simulated ambience are **NOT authentic recordings** of specific places, humans, devices, or events.
- No NASA or other source recordings are implied in science labels.
